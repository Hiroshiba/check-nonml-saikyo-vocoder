- Artificial inputs: fixed six-condition, four-input-seed experiment (24 inputs), generated by the supplied clock-event experiment code. No natural-speech recordings.
- Mean variation model: 平均変動対応版(v2), original saved candidate measurements.
- Coupling model: 相互作用対応版(v3), completed 24-encode / 96-decode run.
- PML and WORLD: original v1 saved measurements through the repository's vocoderbench.pml and vocoderbench.world interfaces. WORLD uses Harvest analysis. These are historical references, not new v3 measurements. Their original source/version hashes are retained in the compressed results.
- Experiment repository: Hiroshiba/check-nonml-saikyo-vocoder.
- Display tooling: Python, NumPy and Matplotlib. Audio files are PCM16 format copies of original float32 WAVs; no normalization, clipping, resynthesis or acoustic remeasurement.

PML/WORLD have no defined conditional Gaussian KL here. Original results and research payloads are not modified or republished as new measurements.
