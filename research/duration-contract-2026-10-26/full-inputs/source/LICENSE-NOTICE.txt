LibriSpeech test-clean, OpenSLR SLR12.
Authors: Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, Sanjeev Khudanpur.
Source: https://www.openslr.org/12/
Archive: https://www.openslr.org/resources/12/test-clean.tar.gz
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Citation: LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books, ICASSP 2015.
Processing: selected the same previously fixed 120 utterances; decoded the full FLAC
signals to bit-exact signed PCM16 and wrote canonical mono 16 kHz WAV containers.
No gain adjustment, resampling, truncation, synthesis, ASR, or quality scoring.
Historical source selection and transcripts are copied from the fixed input contract.
The corpus license is distinct from any repository code license.
